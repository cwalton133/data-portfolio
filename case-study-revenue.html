Revenue Optimization Analytics

Retail / Sales Intelligence Case Study

📉 Problem

The company lacked visibility into sales performance across regions, resulting in missed revenue opportunities.

🧠 Approach

  • Integrated multi-source sales data
  • Built revenue segmentation model
  • Developed interactive Power BI dashboards

💻 Sample SQL Logic

SELECT region, SUM(revenue) AS total_revenue, AVG(order_value) AS avg_order_value FROM sales GROUP BY region ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
